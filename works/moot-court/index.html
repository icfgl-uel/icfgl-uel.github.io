Competition Recap

The Gender Justice Moot Court 2026 marked the inaugural season with the participation of over 500 students forming 131 teams from 24 universities and academies across Vietnam. These numbers not only reflect the reach of a national-scale academic platform but also highlight the growing interest of young legal professionals in gender justice within the context of digital judicial transformation.

Through the competition rounds, contestants had the opportunity to apply legal knowledge, analyze case files, construct arguments, use evidence, and practice advocacy skills in a simulated litigation environment.

Competition Journey

The GJMC 2026 journey unfolded across three main stages: the Preliminary Round, the Semi-Final Round, and the Final Round. Each round progressively raised the bar for expertise, skills, and situational response, enabling teams to fully demonstrate their research and advocacy capabilities.

Preliminary Round

Lawsuit Petition & Evidence Submission

131 teams independently drafted and submitted their Lawsuit Petitions along with supporting evidence via the UEL E-Moot Platform. The top 4 highest-scoring teams advanced to the Semi-Final.

100% Online
Semi-Final Round

Evidence Disclosure, Access & Mediation

4 teams participated in a live, simulated Meeting for the Disclosure and Access of Evidence, and Mediation in accordance with the 2015 Civil Procedure Code (amended 2025) under the direction of a presiding Judge.

100% Online · E-Moot Platform
Final Round · 20/09/2026

First-Instance Trial at UEL

Teams HTND and SHE&CITE competed directly in the Final Round in the form of a first-instance trial at the University of Economics and Law, VNU-HCM — where teams showcased their argumentation, evidence use, cross-examination, and gender justice approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

After the GJMC 2026 has officially concluded, key information about the competition results, teams, awards, photos, and journey through the rounds is compiled here.

Preliminary & Semi-Final Rounds were conducted 100% online via the UEL E-Moot Platform. The Final Round was held in-person at the University of Economics and Law (UEL).
131 teams from 24 universities and academies across Vietnam, comprising over 500 students.
Champion: Team HTND (UEL, VNU-HCM).
Runner-up: Team SHE&CITE (National Economics University).
Co-Third Prize: Teams SYNERGY and EXPECTO PATRONUM.
Special awards were also given for Best Oral Advocate and Best Statement of Claim.
Each team consisted of 04 members. Each student was only allowed to participate in one team.
Teams were evaluated based on: legal mindset, gender equality perspective, presentation & debating skills, evidence disclosure & evaluation skills, legal drafting skills, and reasoning skills.
The competition was hosted and operated by the University of Economics and Law (UEL), VNU-HCM, as part of the Feminist Legal Theory Project supported by the Rosa Luxemburg Stiftung.
Vietnamese was the official language of the competition.
Competition rules and materials were submitted through the UEL E-Moot Platform. The competition rulebook can be downloaded from the platform.
