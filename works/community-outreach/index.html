PWG Project · Call 2

Promoting Decent Work for Women through Climate-Resilient Shrimp-Seaweed Farming in Ca Mau

We are selected to receive funding under the second call of the PWG Project implemented by Oxfam in Vietnam.

Through this support, we are implementing an initiative in Dam Doi Commune, Ca Mau Province, aimed at promoting decent work and climate-resilient livelihoods for low-income women.

The initiative focuses on piloting an integrated model of extensive shrimp farming and Gracilaria seaweed cultivation on floating rafts, while also strengthening women’s capacities, market linkages, and participation in household and community economic decision-making.

Project Focus

An integrated, women-centred livelihood model

The initiative connects climate resilience, local livelihoods, and women’s participation through one practical community-based model.
